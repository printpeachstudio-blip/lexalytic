A question I get asked often: "Should we build it or buy it?"

The honest answer depends on one thing: how specific is your process?

If your sales pipeline looks like a standard pipeline, HubSpot handles it well. If your scheduling is standard, Calendly is fine.

But if your process is specific — your terminology, your workflow, your data — every generic tool requires compromise. And compromise accumulates quietly.

The businesses that benefit most from custom software are the ones that identified one process where the friction of a generic tool was costing more than a custom build would.

That calculation is almost always closer than people expect. Worth doing before renewing another SaaS subscription.
